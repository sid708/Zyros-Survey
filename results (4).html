Would a home care robot actually help you? — Results

Responses on daily needs, trust in paid caregivers, and willingness to use a home care robot — mostly answered by adult children on behalf of an aging parent.

54 of 96 respondents (56%) said they'd be likely or very likely to use a home care robot instead of, or alongside, a hired caregiver — if it cost meaningfully less than a full-time human caregiver and could be trusted more reliably than one. Concerns about neglect, carelessness, or being taken advantage of by paid help came up more often than worries about physical safety itself.
